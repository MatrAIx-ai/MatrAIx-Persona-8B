# Meridian Mobile billing dispute

Your latest Meridian Mobile invoice is higher than you expected, and there is a
charge on it you do not recognise.

Contact Meridian Mobile support and work out what happened. Ask about the charge,
react naturally to what support tells you, and keep going until you can tell
whether you have been given a clear path to getting it sorted out.

Have at least two back-and-forth exchanges (four or more messages in total). Do
not accept a credit or refund you have not been able to verify.
